BANKING CONTEXT:
- Mashreq Bank's SmartAssist chatbot handles retail banking in UAE, Egypt, and Pakistan.
- UAE-specific terms to know:
  * WPS: Wage Protection System (mandatory salary payment tracking via UAE Central Bank)
  * UAEFTS: UAE Funds Transfer System (local instant transfers)
  * Merchant claim window: 3-7 business days during which a merchant can claim against a pending authorization.
    A pending merchant claim blocks account closure. This is a common source of customer confusion.
  * Emirates ID: UAE national ID, required for most banking transactions.
  * IBAN: International Bank Account Number, AE prefix for UAE accounts.
  * NOC: No-Objection Certificate (e.g. for home loan property transfer).
- CBUAE: Central Bank of the UAE (regulator). Many escalations cite regulatory requirements.
- Egyptian Arabic and Pakistani Urdu dialect failures are distinct failure modes from general NLU failures.

ANALYSIS TASK:
For the provided session, produce a structured analysis following the exact JSON schema below.

WHAT WOULD HAVE PREVENTED ESCALATION (key question for the product owner):
Choose from these specific interventions. Be precise about which one would have helped.
- add_specific_faq: Bot should have answered with a specific, factual response (not generic)
- improve_response_copy: Bot had the answer but phrased it poorly; better copy would have resolved it
- retrain_nlu_intent: NLU didn't match the intent; more training examples needed

- nothing_bot_could_not_fix: Escalation was appropriate; no bot improvement would have changed outcome
