REMEDIATION TAXONOMY:
You may recommend one or more types. Order them by expected impact (highest first).

- faq_addition:
  Add new FAQ entries to the bot's knowledge base.
  Appropriate when: customers ask factual questions the bot cannot answer, agent gives standard explanations.
  Impact estimate: HIGH if clustered agent explanations are available; LOW if issues are varied.

- content_quality_improvement:
  Improve existing bot response copy — make responses more specific, actionable, and clear.
  Appropriate when: bot HAD the right information but communicated it poorly.
  The "bot_has_data_communicates_poorly" failure pattern always maps here.
  Impact estimate: HIGH and FAST — this is pure copy/content work, no engineering needed.

- intent_model_improvement:
  Retrain or augment the NLU model with more training examples for this intent.
  Appropriate when: bot_fallback_exhausted is the primary failure mode.
  Impact estimate: MEDIUM — requires NLU team involvement.

- flow_redesign:
  Redesign the conversation flow for this journey — change the sequence of questions, confirmations, or branches.
  Appropriate when: the flow forces customers into dead ends or misses branching points.
  Impact estimate: MEDIUM — requires design and development effort.

- authentication_friction_reduction:
  Reduce authentication steps or improve fallback for auth failures.
  Appropriate when: authentication_friction is primary failure mode.
  Impact estimate: HIGH impact on affected sessions; requires security team sign-off.

- backend_integration:
  Build or fix a backend API integration required for the bot to complete the action.
  Appropriate when: backend_integration_gap is primary failure mode.
  Impact estimate: HIGH but SLOW — engineering effort required.

- language_dialect_coverage:
  Extend NLU training data for underrepresented Arabic or Urdu dialects.
  Appropriate when: language_dialect_gap is primary failure mode.
  Impact estimate: MEDIUM — requires linguistic expertise.

- product_or_policy_change:
  The escalation is driven by a bank policy that requires human judgment.
  Appropriate when: policy_routed is primary failure mode.
  This is a recommendation to the ops/product team, not to the bot team.
  Impact estimate: LOW for bot team; HIGH if policy can be changed.

- no_action:
  Escalation is appropriate. The bot is working correctly for this journey.
  Appropriate when: all or most escalations are correctly policy_routed.
  Do NOT recommend this unless the data clearly supports it.
